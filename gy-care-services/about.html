About GY Care Services

Real healthcare experience, built into every placement.

A UK-based healthcare staffing agency dedicated to providing reliable, compassionate and professional staff to care homes, hospitals and private clients.

Who we are

Staffing led by people who've worked the floor.

Our service is built on real healthcare experience, with a strong focus on quality care, professionalism and trust. We understand the importance of having the right staff in place to deliver safe and effective care, and we are committed to supporting our clients with dependable staffing solutions.

That experience shapes how we recruit, vet and match every professional we place — because we know what "the right fit" actually looks like on a ward or in a care home.

Our mission

Making a real difference in people's lives.

Our mission is to support healthcare providers by supplying qualified and caring professionals who make a real difference in people's lives. We aim to maintain high standards of care while building long-term relationships with both clients and staff.
