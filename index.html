Where is this plane flying from and to?

How to play

  • Planes cross the map along their real flight paths, but carry no labels.
  • Click a plane to open the guess panel.
  • Search for its origin and destination airport by city, code, or country.
  • Submit your guess: +50 for the right origin, +50 for the right destination, +20 bonus if you nail both.
  • Build a streak by getting full routes correct in a row.
  • Stuck? Hit "Give up" to reveal the answer for no points.
